# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Residents who need simple, non-emergency everyday help nearby, and volunteers aged 18 or older who have small pockets of spare time. The pilot is intended to begin with common-area and phone/video-call support, with community organisations as an early partner audience.

## Product Purpose

Help Nearby connects residents with nearby volunteers for short, practical acts of help. It makes it easier to turn spare 10-, 20-, 30-, or 60-minute windows into useful community support, while helping a person ask for assistance without needing to navigate a complicated service directory.

## Positioning

Help Nearby uses an AI-assisted matching layer to connect a resident's request, preferred format, area, timing, and task with a volunteer's available time and suitability. AI supports triage and matching; human review and clear safety boundaries remain part of the pilot.

## Operating Context

The first experience is a public website for a Singapore pilot. Residents and volunteers register interest through separate Google Forms, with responses stored in Google Sheets for manual coordination while the service is validated. Help may happen by voice/video call or in a nearby common area. The website explains the service and routes people into the appropriate form.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Support request categories include parcel or grocery collection, simple prepaid shopping, device or online guidance, general practical help, and other short suitable tasks.
- Requests can be for later the same day or scheduled 24 hours to 7 days ahead.
- Volunteers can offer 10, 20, 30, or 60 minutes and indicate call, common-area, or either format.
- The pilot is free and non-emergency. A match or fulfilment is not guaranteed.
- Purchases must be prepaid by residents; volunteers never advance or handle money.
- Exclude cash handling, banking, passwords, medication, medical care, transport, heavy lifting, personal care, and emergency requests.
- The website is English-only for the current pilot.

## Brand Commitments

The product name is Help Nearby. The voice should be professional, friendly, clear, and reassuring. The site should feel human and accessible rather than like a marketplace or emergency service. Existing logo assets in `public/` are available, but the visual system may be replaced for this redesign.

## Evidence on Hand

The project contains a working website, live Google Form embed links, Google Forms/Sheets pilot workflow, and the current public deployment at `https://helpnearby-sg.github.io/`. No testimonials, participant counts, or outcome metrics have been supplied; the site must not invent them.

## Product Principles

- Small pockets of time can create meaningful local help.
- Clear boundaries make community help safer and easier to trust.
- The request should feel simple; coordination can stay thoughtful behind the scenes.
- Technology should make human help more accessible, not make it feel automated.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The site must work on mobile and desktop, use readable English, maintain strong contrast, provide visible keyboard focus, and make call/video and common-area options equally understandable. Do not assume residents are highly digitally confident.
